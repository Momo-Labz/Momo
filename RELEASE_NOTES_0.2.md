# MOMO 0.2 prototype

- Adds Cursor and Grok alongside Codex, Claude, and Gemini.
- Adds conservative `NEEDS YOU`, failure, completion, working, and idle session states.
- Adds opt-in macOS notifications for recent urgent sessions.
- Rebuilds the app in Momo Labz's off-white, near-black, coral, block-and-line visual system.
- Keeps the menu-bar footprint to the pixel mark plus at most one count.
- Adds a system-wide Control–Option–Space Quick Ask palette.
- Adds a token-paired, read-only local Desk Display for 480 × 320 screens.
- Includes Raspberry Pi kiosk setup, parametric OpenSCAD, and printable STL body/back/stand files.

Known prototype limits:

- Consumer plan percentages remain blank until provider-supported quota adapters are added; the app does not scrape passwords or fabricate percentages.
- Quick Ask copies the prompt and opens the provider. It does not auto-paste or execute without permission.
- Talk, Dictate, and Hands-free are designed in Settings but intentionally labeled as the next stage.
- The attention parser is conservative and needs provider-specific fixtures for guaranteed production alerts.
- The enclosure dimensions must be checked against the exact LCD board before a final print.
- The DMG is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized.
